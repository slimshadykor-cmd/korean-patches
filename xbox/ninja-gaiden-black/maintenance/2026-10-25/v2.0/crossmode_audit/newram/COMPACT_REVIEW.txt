Shared sprite-table bytes and original embedded art remain unchanged. Private

Harness correction:
The first scroll replay exposed stale alias tracking in the previous harness.
Native contiguous allocation relocates existing physical image pages. Example:
VAe6a000 moved from physical1133000 to1053000, then the allocator reused1133000
for compact art. The old harness wrongly mirrored those writes into e6a000.
The corrected harness tracks physical backing and PTE changes. All four final
results use that correction; no candidate game-code change was needed for it.

Limits:
This is not live xemu, a complete XBE boot, or hardware/GPU/thread execution.
Kernel critical sections are bypassed in the single-threaded replay. The extra
resident-page cost is real in the allocator, while its code VA mapping is
modeled. Cleanup begins by releasing the captured OLD large save-menu art to
model its absence under the new code. This is not a user-facing hotpatch.
